# Alejandro Ascárate — personal research website

Quarto source for `https://aleazk.github.io`.

## First publication

1. Upload the complete contents of this folder to the root of the `aleazk/aleazk.github.io` repository and commit to `main`.
2. Open **Settings → Pages** in the repository.
3. Under **Build and deployment → Source**, select **GitHub Actions**.
4. Open **Actions** and wait for the **Publish website** workflow to finish.
5. Visit `https://aleazk.github.io`.

Every later commit to `main` automatically rebuilds the site.

## Editing

Most text is in `.qmd` files. The design is in `styles.scss`. Publication metadata and links are maintained manually in `publications.qmd`; BibTeX records are also collected in `references.bib`.

## Content decisions in this first version

- Mathematical physics and AI have equal prominence.
- The professional record includes peer-reviewed papers, accepted work, and serious research preprints.
- Superseded and speculative material is isolated under `More → Earlier Work`.
- Book-length manuscripts are isolated under `More → Books & Extended Writing`.
- Music and art have a dedicated destination, but the original media remain on the previous WordPress site until high-quality files are migrated.
